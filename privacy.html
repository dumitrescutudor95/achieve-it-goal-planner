Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

In short: Achieve IT keeps your goals and progress on your device. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The only data that leaves your phone is what is needed to verify a subscription, and it is not linked to your name or email.

Who we are

Achieve IT is developed by Tudor Dumitrescu, an independent developer based in Romania. For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Tudor Dumitrescu is the data controller for the limited processing described below. Contact: CONTACT_EMAIL.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following in its private storage, on your device only:

  • goal titles and descriptions, targets, deadlines and commitments;
  • progress entries you log;
  • reminder times and app settings (language, currency, appearance, first day of the week).

We have no server that receives this data, and we cannot see or access it. It is not backed up by us. Your device's own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include it, depending on your device settings.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Their text is calculated on your device and is not sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your device settings at any time.

Purchases and subscriptions

Achieve IT Pro is an auto-renewing subscription. Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your name, email address or payment details. Apple and Google handle your payment under their own privacy policies.

To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID generated by the app (not your name, email or any account identifier);
  • purchase receipts and transaction details from the App Store or Google Play (product, purchase and expiry dates, store country);
  • basic technical information needed to process the request, such as device platform, operating system and app version, and your IP address.

This data is used only to verify and restore your subscription. It is not used for advertising or tracking and is not combined with any other data. RevenueCat acts as our processor; see its privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy. RevenueCat is based in the United States; transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage statistics.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of personal data.

Sharing and export

Data leaves your device only when you choose to share an achievement image or export your data (Pro), and only to the destination you pick.

Legal basis

Subscription verification through RevenueCat is necessary to provide the service you purchased (GDPR Article 6(1)(b), performance of a contract). Everything else happens on your device and is not processed by us.

How long data is kept

On-device data stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Subscription records held by RevenueCat are kept for as long as needed to provide the subscription and to meet legal obligations, and are then deleted.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Your data → Delete everything removes all goals, progress and settings immediately.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes all data stored by the app.
  • Deleting data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Because your goals stay on your device, you can view, export and delete them directly in the app. For subscription data held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will help. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Romania this is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP, dataprotection.ro).

Children

Achieve IT is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
